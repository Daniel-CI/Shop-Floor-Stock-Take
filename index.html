How to use

  1. Racking, quick way: tap "Change" > "500 – Warehouse" and scan any pallet in the racking. Tap "Location done" when the whole warehouse is finished.
  2. Racking, exact way: scan the bin barcode, then the pallet label. Scanning the next bin marks the previous one done automatically, including empty bins.
  3. Shop floor: all machine areas (101 and 118) count as one location, "Shopfloor". Tap "Change" > Shopfloor, scan every pallet next to the machines, then tap "Location done".
  4. Forgot to change location? When a pallet shows "Should be at…", tap "I'm at…" to correct it.
  5. Label unreadable? Tap "Found" next to the pallet in "Expected pallets here".
  6. After counting, open "Follow-up" and give each difference a reason. Re-import a fresh SAP export before posting.
  7. Go to "Report" to export. On iPhone the share sheet opens, so you can save to Files or send by email or Teams.

Export

Includes an Actions sheet that tells the SAP team what to post for every difference (bin transfer, goods issue, consumption, scrap, PI difference, GR), plus Summary, Matched, Wrong location, Missing, Not in SAP, Re-check, Counters, No HU and the scan log.

Backup and merge

iPhones can clear website data, so back up after each area. If several people count different areas, merge their backups onto one phone before exporting.

SAP data

Replacing the SAP export keeps your scans and re-checks them against the new data. Use a fresh export just before you post anything in SAP, so pallets produced or shipped during the count drop out of the differences.
